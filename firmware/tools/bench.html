The bold line is the frequency taken as the speed, the faint ones its multiples: they should sit on the spectrum's peaks, and no peak should stand below the bold line on its own. The motor's sound repeats once a revolution, so its fundamental is its speed (1 line a revolution), but this motor sounds mostly its even multiples, and its speed and twice it look alike to the spectrum.
